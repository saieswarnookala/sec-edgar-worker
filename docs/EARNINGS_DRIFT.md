# Earnings Drift Strategy (Nov 2026)

**Rule:** buy an S&P 500 stock that beats the market by X% on the day after its earnings release; hold N trading days; up to 10 stocks; idle cash optionally in SPY.
- Single rule: X=5%, N=60. Blend: equal money across 9 versions (X=4/5/6% x N=50/60/70), fixed in advance, never re-weighted.
- Point-in-time S&P 500 members, costs included, Aug 2004 - 2026 (~22 years).

|---|---|---|---|
| 2016-2026 | 21.3% | 15.6% | 14.6% |

| 2008-2009 | -8.6% | +9.3% | -10.3% |

| ~22 years (approx.) | ~15% | ~13% | ~11% |
| Worst drop 2004-2015 | -62.2% | -51.4% | -55.2% |

## 9-version grid, 2016-2026 (SPY 14.6%)

## Why the single rule is luck
Neighbours 12-19%; unseen years 8.8% with -62% drop; beat only 79.7% of random runs in 2004-2015; ~half of profit from ~10 trades. The signal itself beat 100% of 1,000 random runs in 2016-2026.

## Verdict
Signal real but small after costs/taxes. Use the blend. Best in tax-advantaged accounts. Not financial advice.
