      How the numbers are calculated

      TTM (last 12 months) adds up the last four reported quarters. Latest quarter ends —; latest fiscal year ends —.
      P/E = market cap ÷ TTM net profit. Market cap = latest close × shares outstanding on the latest balance sheet.
      ROE = TTM net profit ÷ average shareholders’ equity (now and a year ago), the standard way to match a year of profit with the equity that earned it.
      ROCE = TTM EBIT ÷ average capital employed, where capital employed = total assets − current liabilities.
      Debt (borrowings) leaves out lease obligations, as US accounting rules class operating leases as operating items. Rating agencies often count leases as debt, so debt with leases is shown too.
      Free cash flow = cash from operations − capital spending. Growth is compounded per year (CAGR) over fiscal years. EV = market cap + borrowings − cash.
      Industry comes from the company’s FMP profile, matched to the closest benchmark industry by keyword. Industry figures: P/E is total market cap ÷ total profit of the industry’s profitable firms; margins and ROE are industry aggregates (Damodaran, January 2026).
      Colors are rules of thumb, not advice: compare within an industry, and treat red as “look closer,” not “sell.”
      Data check: — gap between the latest fiscal year’s sales and the sum of its four quarters.
